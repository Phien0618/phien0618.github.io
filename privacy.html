Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Hustle Studio ("we", "us") handles when you visit this website or buy a product, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Purchase information. Payments are processed by Waffo Pancake, our merchant of record. When you buy, Waffo collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details. We do not see or store your full card details. Waffo shares with us the order details needed to deliver your product and provide support (such as your email address, the product purchased, the amount and the date).
  • Support emails. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • Website. This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting provider may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations under its own privacy policy.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver the products you buy and to provide support and refunds.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly opt in.

3. Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers needed to run the store (Waffo Pancake for payments, invoicing and tax; GitHub for hosting; our email provider for support), or when required by law.

4. Retention

Order records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes. Support emails are kept as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable period afterwards.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email chenphien@gmail.com. For data held by Waffo Pancake as merchant of record, you can also contact Waffo directly.

6. Children

Our products are intended for adults running a business. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: chenphien@gmail.com
