What it is

Most planners ask you to fill in a lot. This bundle asks for three things a day: fill 3 boxes, not 30.

You get the same simple system in three places. On your iPad, the ADHD Undated Digital Planner has 591 pages in Light and Dark: year, month, week and day pages, a brain dump, a dopamine menu and rewards, habit and mood trackers. Home is one tap away on every page, and any day is a tap or two from Home. Most days you'll only use the Day page and the Brain dump. The page count is high because every month, week and day has its own linked page; the layouts repeat on purpose, so there is nothing new to learn each day. On paper, the Printable Daily & Weekly Planner Pack uses the same Top 3 system, with Daily Top 3, Brain Dump and Dopamine Menu sheets on 40 undated, low-ink pages, laid out separately for US Letter and A4. Both planners are undated, so a skipped week costs nothing and you never have to buy them again for a new year.

Two extras are new in this bundle. The 2027 calendar is the one dated part: a clean month-per-page calendar with a "This month's Top 3" line, no holidays and no clutter. It comes as 4 printable files (Letter or A4, Sunday or Monday start, 14 pages each) and 1 digital file of 27 pages where you tap a month to jump to it. The dates are computed, not typed by hand. The 12 focus wallpapers put a short, specific nudge on your lock screen ("Brain full? Brain dump.", "Set a timer. Begin."), in big type you can read at a glance, sized for iPhone and Android, with space left for your clock and widgets. There are 4 layouts, 6 light and 6 dark, so iPhone Photo Shuffle keeps them fresh.

Everything comes in one ZIP with folders and a 3-page Start-Here.pdf that tells you which folder to open first.

FAQ

Does it work in GoodNotes and Notability?
Yes. The digital planner and the digital 2027 calendar are PDFs with internal links. Import them like any PDF; in GoodNotes, links work in read-only mode. The Quick-Start-Guide.pdf walks you through it.

Is the planner dated?
No. Both planners are undated, so you can start any day of any year. Only the 2027 calendar has printed dates.

What paper sizes are included?
US Letter and A4 for the printable pack and the printable calendar, each laid out for its own size (A4 is not a shrunken Letter page). Print at "Actual size / 100%".

Do I have to write the dates into the planner's month pages?
The planner's month pages are undated so they never expire; you write the month name once. If you want dates already filled in, use the 2027 calendar: every month page (printable and digital) has the correct dates printed.

I had a download that wouldn't open before. What if that happens?
The files are ordinary PDFs and PNGs in a standard ZIP. On iPhone or iPad, tap the ZIP in the Files app to unzip it. If anything won't open, email us: we'll send the file again, or refund you.
