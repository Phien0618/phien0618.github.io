Guide page 4 · Chapter 1, The 7-Step Onboarding Flow

When onboarding is improvised, small things fall through: the deposit invoice goes out late, the logo files arrive in week three, nobody agreed what "a revision" means. A fixed flow removes those gaps. Each step below has one job and one file or email from this kit.

1 · Signed (day 0). The client says yes. Send your contract or agreement and the deposit invoice. These come from your own paperwork; this kit starts right after.

2 · Welcome (day 0). Email 1 + Welcome Guide. Timeline, how to reach you, payment plan and the next 3 steps, in one PDF.

3 · Questions (days 0–3). Discovery Questionnaire (22 questions for the client's field). If it isn't back in 2–3 working days, send Email 2.

4 · Scope (before kickoff). Scope & Revisions Confirmation. What's included, what isn't, revision rounds and how changes work, signed by both of you.

5 · Kickoff (before the start date). Email 3 + Kickoff Call Agenda for a 30-minute call. Within 24 hours, send the recap (Email 4).

6 · Updates (every week). Email 5 on the same day each week. Email 6 whenever a milestone is ready for approval.

7 · Handoff (delivery day + 1–2 weeks). Email 7 + Project Handoff & Feedback sheet with the final files. One to two weeks later, Email 8 asks for feedback.

Why a kit

The client said yes. Now what? You need to send a welcome, collect answers, agree on scope, run a kickoff call, keep them updated and, at the end, hand everything over and ask for feedback. Writing that from scratch for every client takes hours, and the bits you forget (the deposit, the "not included" list, the logo files you still don't have) come back as problems in week three.

Signed to Kickoff gives you the whole sequence. The questionnaire switches between 5 fields, so a developer asks about domains and logins, and a copywriter asks about tone and reviewers.

The 25-page guide (US Letter and A4, same pages) explains the 7-step flow from "signed" to "handoff", lists all 70 questions, shows how to write deliverables and a "Not included" list, runs through the 30-minute kickoff agenda minute by minute, sets communication rules and covers asking for testimonials honestly (only with written permission). FAQ

Do I need Canva, Notion or an account?
No. client-onboarding-kit.html opens in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox and works offline. The checklist opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

How do I get PDFs to send to my client?
Open a document tab and click Print / Save as PDF, then choose "Save as PDF" in your browser's print window. Only that document prints, on Letter or A4.

Where are my details saved, and could I lose them?
Only in the browser you use, on your own computer, when you click Save. Nothing is uploaded. If you clear your browser data, use private/incognito mode or open the file in a different browser, the form starts empty, so keep the PDFs you send in each client's folder. Your wording is also safe in onboarding-templates.md and onboarding-emails.txt.

Is the Scope & Revisions Confirmation a contract?
No. It's a plain-English project summary that both of you sign, and it says so on the page. Keep your own agreement for legal terms. Nothing in the kit is legal advice.

Can I change the wording?
Yes. The generator's text is fixed so it stays consistent, but onboarding-templates.md and onboarding-emails.txt contain the same documents and emails with [Placeholders], ready to edit in Google Docs, Word or Notion.
