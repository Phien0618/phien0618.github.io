The story

Ring in the New Year with a mystery. At the Pendleworth Countdown Gala in the little town of Larkhaven, Master Clockmaker Ellis Pendleworth was about to announce who inherits the keys to Old Lark, the town clock. The announcement never happened. A blue mug of cocoa, a mantel clock stopped at 9:42 and twelve guests with something to hide: your friends have until midnight to work out who did it.

The Last Countdown is a printable party game built around one promise: it finishes on time. The Host Guide includes a Midnight Edition timetable (9:00 pm to 12:00 am, every segment adding up to 180 minutes) with the reveal at 11:45 and the countdown at 11:59, plus a Family Early Countdown (6:30 to 9:00 pm) for kids, teens and early nights. Three Chime Cards and a cut-out countdown clock keep the rounds moving, and each timetable has a "starting late?" plan.

You play Detective Rowan Hale, so you can host and play at the same time. Each guest gets their own 4-page booklet: a public cover, a secret page, Rounds 1–2, and Round 3 with notes. Each round gives three things to share, two questions to ask named suspects and one small task, so quiet guests always have something to do. Twelve evidence cards arrive four per round, marked with stars so they work in black and white.

The solution is fair: a six-step clue chain where every step is backed by an evidence card or a booklet line, plus a table explaining why each of the other eleven suspects is cleared. It sits in its own file, so you never see it while printing.

It is a cozy mystery: no gore, and no drinking needed. Drinks in the story are punch, sparkling cider and cocoa.

What's in each character booklet

Every booklet is 4 pages: page 1 the public cover (name, role, costume idea, what everyone knows about you); page 2 "Before the game", your secret, read before guests arrive; page 3 Rounds 1–2; page 4 Round 3 plus suspect notes and your theories. Each round gives three things to share, two questions for named suspects and one small task.
