Get ready in about an hour

  1. Unzip the download and open the folder for your paper size. Read 00-START-HERE and 01-Host-Guide. Don't open 99-SOLUTION-Host-Only until the reveal, unless you'd rather know the answer first.
  2. Use the cast table to choose roles for your number of guests: 6–16 in total; up to 12 get their own role, and groups of 13–16 share roles in pairs or play Deputy Detectives. The 6 core roles are always needed. You play Inspector Noel.
  3. Send each guest their invitation: printed, typed in the Invitation Maker or as a phone e-invite.
  4. Print one booklet per player and one set of evidence cards. Print at Actual size / 100%. Black and white is fine.
