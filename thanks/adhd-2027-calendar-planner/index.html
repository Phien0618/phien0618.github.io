How to use it

  1. Pick your file. US or Canada: Letter. UK, Europe or Australia: A4. iPad: the iPad file. Then Sunday or Monday start. All six calendar files have the same 43 pages (41 + bonus pages 42 Brain dump and 43 Minimum version).
  2. Printing: print at 100% / Actual size. To print one month, print its 3 pages: January is pages 5–7, February 8–10, up to December 38–40.
  3. iPad: open the iPad PDF in GoodNotes or Notability (in GoodNotes you can also import from the Files app). Tap a month tab to jump to it; Habits and Reset buttons sit on every month calendar. If a tap draws a line instead of jumping, switch GoodNotes to read-only mode or tap with your finger while the Apple Pencil draws.
  4. Each month: on the 1st, write this month's 3 things; tick habits during the month; on the last day, do the 5-minute month reset and carry forward what's left. Head too full? Use the Brain dump (page 42). Low-energy day? Use your Minimum version list (page 43).
  5. Wallpapers: save a PNG to Photos, then Settings › Wallpaper, or add all 4 to Photo Shuffle. They are 1179 × 2556 px (iPhone 15 size).

No holidays are printed: add yours on the Key Dates page (page 4). Personal use only: use it on all your own devices and print as many copies as you like for yourself, but please don't share or resell the files.
