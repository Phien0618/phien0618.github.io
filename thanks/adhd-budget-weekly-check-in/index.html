How to use it

  1. Pick your size. US Letter in the US and Canada, A4 in the UK, Australia, Europe and elsewhere.
  2. Printing: print at "Actual size / 100%", not "Fit to page". Black & white is fine. To print only some pages, enter a range such as Pages 10–12. Print the wallet cards on thick paper and cut on the dashed lines.
  3. iPad (GoodNotes / Notability): import the two iPad-Pastel files. Tap any side tab, a line on Contents or Plan / Week A / Week B on the Paycheck Index to jump; tap Contents or Index at the bottom of a page to go back. In GoodNotes, tap links in read-only mode.
  4. Each week: set a 10-minute timer and do the Weekly Money Check-In: balances, bills due, your one number, 3 money moves.

Personal use only: print as many copies as you like for yourself and your household. Please don't resell, share or upload the files. Never write full account numbers or passwords in the planner. A planning tool, not financial advice.
