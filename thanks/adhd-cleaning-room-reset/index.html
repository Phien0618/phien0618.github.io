How to use it

  1. Pick your size. US Letter in the US and Canada, A4 almost everywhere else.
  2. Printing: print at "Actual size / 100%", not "Fit to page". Black & white is fine. To print only the room cards, enter Pages 6–13.
  3. iPad (GoodNotes / Notability): import the planner or the 30-day file. Tap a line on Contents (or a day on the Day Index) to jump; tap Contents or Index at the bottom of a page to go back. In GoodNotes, tap links in read-only mode.
  4. Each reset: pick one room, copy any 3 tasks from its Room Card onto a 3-Task Reset page, set a timer, tick, take a reward, stop.

Personal use only: print as many copies as you like for yourself and your household. Please don't resell, share or upload the files. This planner is an organizing tool, not medical advice.
