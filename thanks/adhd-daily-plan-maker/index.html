How to use it (5 minutes)

  1. Save the tool somewhere you'll find it. Put ADHD-Daily-Plan-Maker.html in Documents (not only in Downloads), then double-click it. It opens in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox and works without internet. On iPad: save it to the Files app, then open it in Safari.
  2. Add your routines once. Meds, stand-up, walking the dog: give each one a time of day (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) and the weekdays it happens. Up to 12.
  3. Pick 1, 7, 14 or 30 pages. Leave the start date empty for "Day 1, Day 2…" or enter a date for "Mon 12 Oct". Type up to three things for any day, or leave the boxes blank to write by hand.
  4. Print. Choose US Letter or A4 in the tool and the same paper in the print window, scale 100% (Actual size), headers and footers off. "Save as PDF" also works.
  5. Make a backup. Your setup is saved in this browser only. Tap Export backup now and then; Import backup moves it to another browser or device. Start-Here.pdf explains when you need it (especially on iPad).
