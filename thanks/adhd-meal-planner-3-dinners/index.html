How to use it

  1. Pick your size. US Letter in the US and Canada, A4 in the UK, Australia, Europe and elsewhere.
  2. Printing: print at "Actual size / 100%", not "Fit to page". Black & white is fine. To print only the five Meal Library pages, enter Pages 5–9. Print the meal cards on thicker paper and cut on the dashed lines.
  3. iPad (GoodNotes / Notability): import the planner or the 12-week file. Tap a line on Contents (or Plan / Groceries on the Week Index) to jump; tap Contents or Index at the bottom of a page to go back. In GoodNotes, tap links in read-only mode.
  4. Each week: write 3 dinners on the Weekly Plan, write the grocery list by section, and pick the other nights from the Meal Library or your Safe Meals.

Personal use only: print as many copies as you like for yourself and your household. Please don't resell, share or upload the files. Check labels for allergies; this is a planning tool, not nutrition advice.
