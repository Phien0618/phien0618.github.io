How to use it

  1. Pick your size. US Letter in the US and Canada, A4 almost everywhere else.
  2. Printing: print at "Actual size / 100%", not "Fit to page". Black & white is fine. The wider left margin is for hole punching. Print a week's worth of Daily Top 3 pages (single page 04) and keep them in a binder.
  3. iPad (GoodNotes / Notability): import the full planner PDF, or the 30-day Daily Top 3 file for everyday use. Tap a line on the Contents page (or a day on the Day index) to jump. In GoodNotes, tap links in read-only mode.
  4. Each day: write three things, plus the first tiny step for each. Tick what's done. Anything left goes into Carry forward and starts tomorrow's page. No rewriting.
  5. Feeling stuck? Empty your head on the Brain Dump page, then use Brain Dump → Top 3 to pick three.

Personal use only: print as many copies as you like for yourself and your household. Please don't resell, share or upload the files. This planner is an organizing tool, not medical advice.
