How to use it

  1. Pick your size. US Letter in the US and Canada, A4 in the UK, Australia, Europe and elsewhere.
  2. Start here: Syllabus Dump (page 4). Then give each big assignment a Start-By Planner (page 10).
  3. Printing: print at "Actual size / 100%", not "Fit to page". To print only some pages, enter a range such as Pages 13–14. Print the 127-page semester file one week at a time (Week 1 = Pages 2–8).
  4. iPad (GoodNotes / Notability): import the two iPad-Pastel files. Tap a side tab, a line on Contents or a button on the Semester Index to jump; tap Contents, Index or "Week N plan" at the bottom of a page to go back. If a tap draws a line, switch to read-only / select mode first.

Personal use only: print as many copies as you like for yourself. Please don't resell, share or upload the files. ADHD-friendly by design; not medical advice.
