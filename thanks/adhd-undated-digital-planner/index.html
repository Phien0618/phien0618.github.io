How to start (2 minutes)

  1. On your iPad, download the Light or Dark PDF (or both) and open it with GoodNotes, Notability or Xodo. In GoodNotes you can also use Import from the Files app.
  2. Tap Start here, then on Home tap your month and today's date.
  3. Write three things on that Daily Top 3 page. Anything not done goes into Carry forward.
  4. If a tap draws a line instead of jumping: in GoodNotes, switch to read-only mode or tap with your finger while "Apple Pencil only" drawing is on; in Notability, tap with your finger. The Quick Start Guide shows the steps.

Links may not work in GoodNotes for Windows. Personal use only: use it on all your own devices, but please don't share or resell the files.
