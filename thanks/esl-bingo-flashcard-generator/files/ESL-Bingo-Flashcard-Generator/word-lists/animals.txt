# Animals
cat = a small pet that says "meow"
dog = a pet that barks and wags its tail
fish = an animal that lives and swims in water
bird = an animal with wings and feathers
rabbit = a small animal with long ears that hops
cow = a farm animal that gives us milk
pig = a pink farm animal with a curly tail
duck = a bird that swims and says "quack"
frog = a small green animal that jumps
lion = a big wild cat with a mane
elephant = a big grey animal with a long nose
monkey = an animal that climbs trees and eats bananas
bear = a big strong animal that sleeps in winter
sheep = a farm animal with soft wool
snake = a long animal with no legs
turtle = a slow animal with a hard shell
mouse = a very small animal with a long tail
chicken = a farm bird that lays eggs
owl = a bird with big eyes that is awake at night
bee = a small insect that makes honey
