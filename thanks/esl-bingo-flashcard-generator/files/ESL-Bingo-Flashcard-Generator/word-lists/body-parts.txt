# Body Parts
head = the top part of your body
hair = it grows on your head
face = the front of your head
eye = you see with it
ear = you hear with it
nose = you smell with it
mouth = you eat and talk with it
teeth = white and hard; you bite with them
tongue = it is in your mouth; you taste with it
neck = it is between your head and your body
shoulder = it is at the top of your arm
arm = it is between your shoulder and your hand
elbow = the middle part of your arm that bends
hand = it has five fingers
finger = there are five on each hand
leg = you walk with it
knee = the middle part of your leg that bends
foot = it is at the end of your leg
toe = there are five on each foot
tummy = the front part of your body under your chest
