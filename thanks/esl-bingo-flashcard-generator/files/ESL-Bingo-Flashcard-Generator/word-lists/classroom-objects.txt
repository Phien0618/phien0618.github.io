# Classroom Objects
pencil = you write and draw with it, and you can erase it
pen = you write with it in ink
eraser = it removes pencil marks
ruler = you measure and draw straight lines with it
book = you read it; it has many pages
notebook = you write your notes in it
bag = you carry your books to school in it
desk = a table where a student works
chair = you sit on it
scissors = you cut paper with them
glue = it sticks paper together
crayon = a colored wax stick for drawing
clock = it tells you the time
board = the teacher writes on it at the front of the class
computer = a machine with a screen and a keyboard
door = you open it to go into the room
window = glass in the wall that lets light in
paper = thin white sheets you write on
marker = a thick pen with bright colors
globe = a round map of the world
