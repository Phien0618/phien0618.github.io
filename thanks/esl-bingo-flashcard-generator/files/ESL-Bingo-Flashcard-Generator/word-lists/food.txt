# Food
apple = a round red or green fruit
banana = a long yellow fruit
orange = a round orange fruit with a thick skin
grapes = small round fruits that grow in bunches
strawberry = a small red fruit with tiny seeds outside
watermelon = a big green fruit that is red inside
carrot = a long orange vegetable
tomato = a soft red fruit we use in salads
corn = a yellow vegetable with many small seeds
bread = food made from flour that we use for sandwiches
cheese = a yellow food made from milk
egg = food that comes from a chicken
milk = a white drink that comes from cows
juice = a drink made from fruit
rice = small white grains we cook and eat in a bowl
noodles = long thin food we often eat with soup
pizza = flat bread with cheese and tomato on top
cake = a sweet food for birthdays
cookie = a small sweet baked snack
candy = a small very sweet food
