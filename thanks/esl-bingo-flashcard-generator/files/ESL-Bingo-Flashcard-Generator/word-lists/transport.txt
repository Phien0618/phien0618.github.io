# Transport
bus = a big vehicle that carries many people
car = a vehicle with four wheels for a family
taxi = a car you pay to ride in
train = it runs on tracks and has many carriages
plane = it flies in the sky
boat = a small vehicle that moves on water
ship = a very big boat
bike = it has two wheels and pedals
motorbike = a bike with an engine
truck = a big vehicle that carries things
helicopter = it flies and has a big spinning blade on top
rocket = it flies into space
tractor = a farm vehicle with big wheels
scooter = you stand on it and push with one foot
van = a vehicle bigger than a car for carrying things
ambulance = it takes sick people to the hospital
submarine = a boat that goes under the water
balloon = a big bag of hot air that flies with a basket
tram = a train that runs on the street in a city
skateboard = a board with four small wheels
