# Weather
sunny = the sun is shining
cloudy = there are many clouds in the sky
rainy = water is falling from the clouds
snowy = snow is falling and it is white outside
windy = the air is moving fast
stormy = there is rain, wind and thunder
foggy = the air is grey and you cannot see far
rainbow = colored arcs in the sky after rain
hot = a very high temperature
cold = a very low temperature
umbrella = it keeps you dry in the rain
raincoat = a coat that keeps you dry
boots = tall shoes for rain or snow
scarf = you wear it around your neck when it is cold
gloves = you wear them on your hands when it is cold
snowman = a figure made of snow
puddle = a small pool of water on the ground
thermometer = it tells you the temperature
lightning = a bright flash of light in a storm
tornado = a very strong spinning wind
