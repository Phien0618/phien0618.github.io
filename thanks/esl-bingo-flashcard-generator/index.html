Start in 2 minutes

  1. Unzip the ZIP (or download Bingo-Flashcard-Generator.html on its own). After this you don't need internet.
  2. Double-click Bingo-Flashcard-Generator.html. It opens in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  3. Word List tab: paste your words, one per line, word = meaning, or load a built-in sample list.
  4. Bingo tab: choose 3×3, 4×4 or 5×5, the number of cards and a seed (e.g. unit-3) so you can reprint the same cards later.
  5. Flashcards tab: choose front, back and cards per page. Pick long-edge, short-edge or fold mode.
  6. Choose A4 or US Letter and click Print / Save as PDF. Print at 100% scale with background graphics on. For double-sided flashcards, check that the A1/A2… tags line up on your first sheet.
  7. No time? Print one of the ready-to-print PDFs. The Quick Start PDF has the print settings on one page.
