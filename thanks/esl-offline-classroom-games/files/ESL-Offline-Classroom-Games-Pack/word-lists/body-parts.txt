# Body parts
head = the top part of your body
eye = you see with it
ear = you hear with it
nose = you smell with it
mouth = you eat and talk with it
tooth = white and hard; you bite with it
hand = it has five fingers
finger = you have ten of them
arm = between your shoulder and your hand
leg = you walk with it
foot = you stand on it
knee = the middle of your leg
shoulder = between your neck and your arm
hair = it grows on your head
