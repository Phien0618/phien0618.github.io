# Classroom objects
pencil = you write and draw with it
eraser = it removes pencil marks
ruler = you draw straight lines with it
scissors = you cut paper with them
glue = it makes paper stick together
notebook = you write your lessons in it
backpack = you carry your books in it
desk = a table where you sit and work
chair = you sit on it
whiteboard = the teacher writes on it with markers
marker = a thick pen for the whiteboard
crayon = a stick of colour for drawing
book = you read it
pencil case = a small bag for pens and pencils
