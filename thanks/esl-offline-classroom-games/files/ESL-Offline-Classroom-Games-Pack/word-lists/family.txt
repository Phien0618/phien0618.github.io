# Family
mother = your mum
father = your dad
sister = a girl with the same parents as you
brother = a boy with the same parents as you
grandmother = your mum's or dad's mother
grandfather = your mum's or dad's father
aunt = your mum's or dad's sister
uncle = your mum's or dad's brother
cousin = your aunt's or uncle's child
baby = a very young child
son = a boy child of a parent
daughter = a girl child of a parent
