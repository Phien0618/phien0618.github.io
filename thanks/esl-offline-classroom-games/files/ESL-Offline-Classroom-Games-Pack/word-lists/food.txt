# Fruit
apple = a round red or green fruit
banana = a long yellow fruit
orange = a round fruit and a colour
# Vegetables
carrot = a long orange vegetable
potato = we make chips and fries from it
tomato = a red fruit we use in salads
# Drinks
milk = a white drink from cows
juice = a drink made from fruit
water = a clear drink we need every day
# Meals
breakfast = the first meal of the day
lunch = the meal in the middle of the day
dinner = the meal in the evening
# Snacks
cookie = a small sweet baked snack
popcorn = a snack we eat at the cinema
ice cream = a cold sweet dessert
