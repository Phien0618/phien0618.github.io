# Transport
car = a vehicle with four wheels for a family
bus = a big vehicle that carries many people
train = it runs on rails
plane = it flies in the sky
bike = two wheels; you pedal it
boat = it moves on water
taxi = a car you pay to ride in
subway = a train that runs under the city
helicopter = it flies with spinning blades on top
truck = a big vehicle that carries things
motorcycle = a bike with an engine
ship = a very big boat
