# Daily routine
wake up = open your eyes in the morning
get up = get out of bed
brush your teeth = clean your teeth
take a shower = wash your body with water
get dressed = put on your clothes
eat breakfast = have the first meal of the day
go to school = travel to school
have lunch = eat in the middle of the day
do homework = study at home after school
play = have fun with friends or toys
watch TV = look at shows on television
cook dinner = make the evening meal
wash your face = clean your face with water
go to bed = go to sleep at night
