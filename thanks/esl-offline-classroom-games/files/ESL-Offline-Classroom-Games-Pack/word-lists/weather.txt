# Weather
sunny = the sun is shining
rainy = water is falling from the sky
cloudy = there are lots of clouds
windy = the air is moving fast
snowy = white snow is falling
stormy = rain, wind and thunder
foggy = it is hard to see far
hot = a very high temperature
cold = a very low temperature
rainbow = colours in the sky after rain
umbrella = it keeps you dry in the rain
thunder = a loud noise in a storm
