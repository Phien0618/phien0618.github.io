Start in 2 minutes

  1. Unzip the ZIP. Copy the folder to your classroom computer or a USB stick if you like. After this you don't need internet.
  2. Double-click START-HERE.html. It opens in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  3. Paste your vocabulary into the box, one entry per line: word = meaning, or two columns copied from Excel / Google Sheets. Lines starting with # become Quiz Board categories. To try it first, open any file in word-lists/, copy everything and paste it in.
  4. Click any game. Inside a game, the Games button switches to another game and keeps your list. Keys: Space (start / next), F (full screen), Esc (close), ? (rules and keys).
  5. For game-by-game steps and classroom variations, open the Teacher Guide (US Letter or A4).
