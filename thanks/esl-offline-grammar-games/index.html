How to use it

  1. Unzip the ZIP (or download the HTML file). Copy the folder to your laptop or a USB stick.
  2. Double-click ESL-Grammar-Games.html. It opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari with no internet. In Safari, right-click the file and choose Open With → Safari.
  3. Tick one or more grammar points (or your own set), pick a game, choose 2–6 teams and press F for full screen.
  4. To add your own sentences, open My Sentences and paste one per line: She *has lived* here since 2019. | lived, has live, is living
  5. Your saved sets live in this browser's local storage. Press Export .txt before clearing the browser cache or moving to another computer, then Import .txt there.
  6. Read the 1-page Quick Start first; the Teacher Guide has the rules, 2 variations per game and a 45-minute sample lesson.
