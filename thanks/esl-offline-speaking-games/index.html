How to use it

  1. Unzip the ZIP to your computer or a USB stick.
  2. Double-click START-HERE.html. It opens in your browser with no internet: Chrome or Edge 87+, Firefox 75+ or Safari 14.1+ (not Internet Explorer).
  3. Click a game, choose the level (A1–A2 or B1–B2), connect the projector and press Space. Press ? in any game to see every shortcut.
  4. Want your own prompts? Open Prompts in the top bar, paste them in, then Save list. Lists live only in this browser: use Export .txt to keep a backup before clearing browser data.
  5. Print docs/Quick-Start (1 page) for your desk, and docs/Prompt-Bank if you ever teach without a projector.
