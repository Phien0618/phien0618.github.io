Faceless Shorts Performance Tracker: YouTube, TikTok & Reels
Faceless Creator Kit
==========================================================

For reviewing performance after you post. It is not a posting calendar and not a script tool.

FILES (4, also bundled in Faceless-Shorts-Performance-Tracker_FacelessCreatorKit.zip)
1. Faceless-Shorts-Performance-Tracker.xlsx
   The tracker. 7 tabs: Start Here, Settings, Idea Bank, Video Log,
   Revenue Estimator, Dashboard, Weekly Review. 1,510 formulas, fictional
   sample data (30 ideas, 36 Video Log rows), no macros.
2. Quick-Start-Guide_Letter.pdf   3-page guide, US Letter
3. Quick-Start-Guide_A4.pdf       the same guide, A4
4. README.txt                     this file

SUPPORTED APPS
- Excel 2016 or later, Excel 365 (Windows and Mac)
- Google Sheets: upload the .xlsx to Drive, open it, File > Save as Google Sheets
Uses only functions available in Excel 2016 and Google Sheets (no XLOOKUP, FILTER, LET or dynamic arrays). All formulas recalculated with zero errors in our formula checker.

GOOD TO KNOW
- One row per post: 6 quick labels + 7 numbers from your analytics
  (2 optional affiliate fields).
- Light yellow cells = you type. Light grey cells = formulas.
- Baseline = average 7-day views of your OTHER posts on the same platform
  and length bucket (at least 3 others, otherwise "Need 3+").
  1.50x or more = Winner (green), 0.50x or less = Flop (red).
- Dashboard "Same video across platforms": latest 12 titles with views,
  verdict and best platform.
- Pre-filled formula rows: 60 (rows 5–64) ≈ 4 weeks at 5 videos/week on 3 platforms. To log more, copy row 64 and paste down — about 10 seconds.
- Dropdowns, colours and all summaries already cover rows 5–1000.
- Dashboard and Weekly Review are locked with NO password
  (Excel: Review > Unprotect Sheet). Keep / Stop / Test stay editable.
- Clear the sample data: see the Start Here tab for the exact ranges.
