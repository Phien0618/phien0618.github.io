How to use it

  1. Open the file. Excel 2016 or later: just open the .xlsx. Google Sheets: upload it to Google Drive, open it and choose File > Save as Google Sheets.
  2. Look around with the sample data first. It is fictional (30 ideas, 36 Video Log rows) so you can see every formula working. Then clear it: the Start Here tab lists the exact ranges (Video Log A5:O40, Idea Bank, Revenue Estimator, Weekly Review notes, Settings B5:B8).
  3. Settings. Enter your channel name, start date (a Monday), weekly goal (posts × platforms, e.g. 5 videos × 3 platforms = 15) and currency. Edit the Niches and Hook types lists to match your channel.
  4. Video Log. About 7 days after posting, add one row per video per platform: 6 quick labels (date, title, niche, hook type, platform, length) and 7 numbers from your analytics, plus the 2 optional affiliate fields. Light yellow = you type; grey = formulas.
  5. Dashboard + Weekly Review. Read the verdicts, the ★ Best rows, the Top 5 and the Same video across platforms table. Once a week, write Keep / Stop / Test notes in Weekly Review.

Need more than 60 rows? Copy row 64 and paste down — about 10 seconds. Dropdowns, colours and all summaries already cover rows 5–1000. Dashboard and Weekly Review are locked with no password (Excel: Review > Unprotect Sheet).
