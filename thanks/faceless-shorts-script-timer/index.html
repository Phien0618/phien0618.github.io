How to use it

  1. Open the tool. Save Shorts-Script-Builder.html somewhere you'll find it on your computer and double-click it. It opens in your browser and works offline.
  2. Build. Pick a niche, a hook type and a length (15, 30, 45 or 60 s), type your topic and press Build script. Press Load worked example to see a finished script.
  3. Time & Edit. Set your speed (130 / 150 / 170 / 180 wpm or custom 100–220) and rewrite each line. Type // for a 0.4 s pause. Red means a segment runs more than 10 percent long.
  4. Captions. Choose 1 to 5 words per caption or one per sentence, then Download .srt. Timings are estimates: nudge them after you add the voiceover.
  5. Library. Save the script as Draft, Recorded or Posted. Export CSV for your own tracker.
  6. Batch & My hooks. Paste up to 30 topics to create Drafts at once, and add or import your own hooks (CSV columns niche, hook_type, template).

Back up your library. Saved scripts live in your browser on this device. Press Backup .json in the Library tab now and then, and Restore .json to bring everything back on any browser or device.

Updates and fixes. If you find a bug, email us. Fixed versions go on this same page, free for you. Before you swap in a new version, press Backup .json; if the library looks empty in the new file, press Restore .json.
