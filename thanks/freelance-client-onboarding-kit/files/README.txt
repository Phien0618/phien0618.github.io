SIGNED TO KICKOFF · FREELANCE CLIENT ONBOARDING KIT · README
=============================================================

OPEN FIRST: client-onboarding-kit.html
Double-click it to open it in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
Click "Load example" to see a finished set, then "Reset" and type your own
client's details once. Works offline: no account, no Canva, no internet needed.

THE 7 FILES
1. client-onboarding-kit.html      the generator: 5 documents + 8 emails from one form.
                                   Letter/A4 switch, Print / Save as PDF per document,
                                   Copy button per email, Save keeps your details in
                                   this browser.
2. onboarding-guide-letter.pdf     the guide, US Letter: the 7-step onboarding flow,
                                   all 70 discovery questions, 4 printable blank forms
                                   and a worked example
3. onboarding-guide-a4.pdf         the same guide, A4
4. onboarding-templates.md         the 5 documents as plain text with [Placeholders]
                                   and all 70 questions, for Google Docs, Word or Notion
5. onboarding-emails.txt           the 8 emails as plain text (§1–§8), each with a
                                   Subject line
6. client-onboarding-checklist.xlsx  3 tabs: Start Here, Checklist (20 steps × 8
                                   clients, completion %), Clients (20 rows)
7. README.txt                      this file

THE 5 DOCUMENTS THE GENERATOR MAKES
Welcome Guide · Discovery Questionnaire · Scope & Revisions Confirmation ·
Kickoff Call Agenda · Project Handoff & Feedback

PRINT OR SAVE AS PDF
Pick Letter or A4 at the top, open a document tab, click "Print / Save as PDF".
Only that document prints. In the print window choose "Save as PDF" to email it.
Tip: turn off "Headers and footers" in the print window for a clean page.

GOOGLE SHEETS (for the checklist)
Google Drive > New > File upload > choose the .xlsx, then open it with Google Sheets.
Or in Sheets: File > Import > Upload > choose the file > Replace spreadsheet.
Nothing is locked: rename steps or add rows as you like.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Your details are saved only in your own browser (click Save). Clearing your
  browser data or using another browser starts empty.
- Empty fields show as [Placeholders] so nothing is silently left out.
- The Scope & Revisions Confirmation is a project summary, not a legal contract.
  Nothing in this kit is legal advice.
