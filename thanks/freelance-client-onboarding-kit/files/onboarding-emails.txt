SIGNED TO KICKOFF · THE 8 ONBOARDING EMAILS
===========================================

Plain-text versions of the 8 emails in client-onboarding-kit.html.
Replace every [Bracket Placeholder] with your details, or use the HTML
generator: it fills them in for you and has a Copy button on each email.
Lines with "…" are for you to complete before sending.

Placeholders used:
[Client Name] = the client's first name (greeting) · [Client Company]
[Project Name] · [Start Date] · [Delivery Date] · [Deposit] · [Balance]
[Milestone] / [Milestone Date] = the next milestone · [Deliverable]
[Not Included] · [Revision Rounds] · [Hourly Rate] · [Payment Days]
[Your Name] · [Business Name] · [Your Email] · [Your Website]
[Channel] · [Reply Hours] · [Working Hours] · [Time Zone]


§1 WELCOME & NEXT STEPS
Send: the day the client says yes (with the Welcome Guide, questionnaire
and scope confirmation attached).
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: Welcome! Next steps for [Project Name]

Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for choosing [Business Name] for [Project Name]. I'm looking forward to starting on [Start Date].

Attached is your Welcome Guide. It covers the timeline, how to reach me and the payment plan. To get us started, there are three quick steps:

1. Pay the deposit of [Deposit] to reserve your start date.
2. Fill in the Discovery Questionnaire (attached, about 20 minutes).
3. Review and sign the Scope & Revisions Confirmation (attached).

The best way to reach me is [Channel] ([Your Email]). I reply within [Reply Hours] hours on working days ([Working Hours], [Time Zone]).

Talk soon,
[Your Name]
[Business Name] · [Your Website]


§2 QUESTIONNAIRE REMINDER
Send: 2–3 working days after §1 if the questionnaire hasn't come back.
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: Quick reminder: your questionnaire for [Project Name]

Hi [Client Name],

Just a friendly reminder about the Discovery Questionnaire for [Project Name]. Your answers shape everything I make, so I'd like to have them before our kickoff call.

It takes about 20 minutes. If a question doesn't apply, write "n/a" and move on. If it's easier to talk it through, reply and we'll cover it on the call.

Could you send it back within the next two working days? That keeps us on track for [Start Date].

Thanks,
[Your Name]
[Business Name] · [Your Website]


§3 KICKOFF CALL INVITE
Send: once the questionnaire and signed scope are back.
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: Let's book our 30-minute kickoff call for [Project Name]

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for sending everything over. The next step is a 30-minute kickoff call before we start on [Start Date].

On the call we'll go over your goals, a few follow-ups to your questionnaire, the scope (what's included and what isn't), the timeline, and how we'll keep in touch. The agenda is attached.

Please reply with two or three 30-minute slots that work for you, and I'll send a calendar invite. If someone else will approve the work, please invite them too.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Business Name] · [Your Website]

§8 FEEDBACK & TESTIMONIAL REQUEST
Send: 1–2 weeks after handoff, once the client has used the work.
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: 3 quick questions about [Project Name]

Hi [Client Name],

Now that [Project Name] is wrapped up, I'd love your honest feedback. It takes about 5 minutes:

1. What went well in working together?
2. What one thing could I have done better?
3. How has the result helped [Client Company] so far, or how do you expect it to?

If you're happy for me to quote your answers publicly (with your name and company), please say so in your reply. If you don't, they stay private. Either answer is completely fine.

And if you know someone who could use similar help, I'd be grateful for an introduction.

Thank you again,
[Your Name]
[Business Name] · [Your Website]
