# Signed to Kickoff · The 5 Onboarding Documents (plain-text templates)

Plain-text versions of the 5 documents that client-onboarding-kit.html builds for you. Paste one into Google Docs, Word or Notion, replace every `[Bracket Placeholder]`, and send it. The HTML generator fills the placeholders in for you; use these when you want to edit the wording freely.

Placeholders: [Your Name] · [Business Name] · [Your Email] · [Your Website] · [Working Hours] · [Time Zone] · [Reply Hours] · [Channel] · [Payment Days] · [Deposit %] · [Revision Rounds] · [Hourly Rate] · [Client Name] · [Client Company] · [Project Name] · [Start Date] · [Delivery Date] · [Project Fee] · [Deposit] · [Balance] · [Deliverable] · [Not Included] · [Milestone] · [Milestone Date]

Deposit = Project Fee × Deposit %. Balance = Project Fee − Deposit. Example: 2,700 × 50% = 1,350, balance 2,700 − 1,350 = 1,350.

## Discovery Questionnaire

**[Business Name]** · [Your Email] · [Your Website]
DISCOVERY QUESTIONNAIRE · Prepared for [Client Name] · [Client Company]

**Discovery Questionnaire**

For [Project Name]. Please answer in your own words; short answers are fine. It takes about 20 minutes. If a question doesn't apply, write "n/a". Send it back to [Your Email] before our kickoff call.

Use the 10 general questions plus the 12 questions for your field: 22 questions in total. Delete the four field sets you don't need.

### About your business (general) · 10

- G1. In one or two sentences, what does your business do, and for whom?
- G2. What is the main goal of this project? What should be different when it's finished?
- G3. How will you know the project was a success? Name one or two signs you'll look for.
- G4. Who are your ideal customers or readers? Describe them as specifically as you can.
- G5. Who are your main competitors or alternatives, and what do you like or dislike about them?
- G6. Who will give feedback and approve the work? Who has the final say?
- G7. Are there any fixed dates, launches or events this project must be ready for?
- G8. What have you already tried or made for this, and what did or didn't work?
- G9. What files, logins or materials will you share with me, and by when?
- G10. Is there anything that worries you about this project, or anything you'd like me to avoid?

### Design questions · 12

- D1. Which three words should people use to describe your brand after seeing the new design?
- D2. Which three words should they never use?
- D3. Share 2–3 brands or designs you admire (in any industry). What do you like about each?
- D4. Is there anything in your current visual identity you want to keep (colours, symbol, typeface)?
- D5. Are there colours you love, or colours you want to avoid? Why?
- D6. Where will the design be used most: website, social media, packaging, print, signage, or somewhere else?
- D7. Do you have existing brand guidelines, logo files or fonts? Please list what you can send.
- D8. Do you prefer a wordmark, a symbol, or both together? Or would you like my recommendation?
- D9. Which sizes and formats do you need for the final files (for example print, web, social profiles)?
- D10. Are there legal names, taglines or trademarks that must appear exactly as written?
- D11. Who will use the final files day to day, and which software do they use?
- D12. Is there any imagery, style or symbol that would be inappropriate for your audience or culture?

### Web Development questions · 12

- W1. What is the website's main job: sell, generate leads, inform, or something else?
- W2. Which platform are you on now, or want to use (for example WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, custom code)?
- W3. Who owns the domain and hosting, and can you invite me as a user?
- W4. Which accounts will I need (CMS, hosting, DNS, payments, analytics, email)? Please share access securely, not in a plain email.
- W5. Which pages or features must be ready at launch, and which can wait for a later phase?
- W6. Who will write and supply the text, images and product data, and by when?
- W7. Which third-party tools must the site connect to (payments, booking, newsletter, CRM)?
- W8. Which browsers and devices matter most to your customers?
- W9. Are there accessibility, privacy or legal requirements (cookie notice, terms, accessibility standards)?
- W10. Who will update the site after launch, and what do they need to do without a developer?
- W11. Do you have analytics or traffic data from the current site that I can look at?
- W12. How should the launch happen: on a set date, as a quiet launch, or after a full backup of the old site?

### Writing & Copy questions · 12

- C1. What is the piece (or set of pieces) for, and what should the reader do after reading it?
- C2. Who is the reader? What do they already know, and what do they doubt?
- C3. Describe the tone you want in three words. Is there a tone you want to avoid?
- C4. Share 2–3 pieces of writing you like (yours or someone else's). What works in each?
- C5. Are there words, phrases or claims you always use, or never use?
- C6. Which key facts, numbers or proof points must I include? Where can I check them?
- C7. Who are your competitors, and how should your message sound different from theirs?
- C8. Do you follow a style guide (US or UK spelling, AP, Chicago, a house style)?
- C9. Who will review drafts, and will their comments arrive together in one document?
- C10. Are there SEO keywords, word counts or formats I need to hit?
- C11. Will I interview anyone (you, your team, your customers)? Who, and how do I reach them?
- C12. Where will the copy be published, and who will format and upload it?

### Marketing & Social questions · 12

- M1. Which goal matters most for the next 90 days: awareness, leads, sales, or community?
- M2. Which channels are you active on now, and which ones should we focus on?
- M3. What results have you had so far on each channel? Please share any numbers you track.
- M4. Who is your ideal follower or customer, and where do they spend time online?
- M5. Which competitors or accounts do you watch, and what do you think they do well?
- M6. What are your key offers, launches or promotions in the next 3 months?
- M7. What content do you already have (photos, videos, blog posts, customer quotes you have permission to use)?
- M8. Who approves posts or campaigns before they go live, and how much notice do they need?
- M9. What is your monthly budget for paid ads, if any, and who owns the ad account?
- M10. Which topics, words or images are off-limits for your brand?
- M11. Which accounts will I need access to, and at what permission level?
- M12. How do you want results reported to you, and how often?

### Consulting questions · 12

- K1. What is the specific problem or decision you want help with?
- K2. What would a good outcome look like 3 months after we finish?
- K3. What have you already tried to solve it, and what happened?
- K4. Who are the key people involved, and who makes the final decision?
- K5. Which data, reports or documents can you share before we start?
- K6. What constraints should I know about (budget, people, time, policies)?
- K7. Which parts of the business are off-limits or sensitive in this project?
- K8. How involved do you want to be: regular working sessions, or a recommendation at the end?
- K9. What format do you want the final output in (report, presentation, workshop, plan)?
- K10. How do you prefer to decide: with data, with options to compare, or with one clear recommendation?
- K11. Are there people who might resist the changes, and how should we involve them?
- K12. How will you measure whether my recommendations worked?

**Anything else you'd like me to know?**
