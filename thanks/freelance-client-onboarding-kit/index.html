Start in 15 minutes

  1. Double-click client-onboarding-kit.html. Click Load example to see a finished set, then Reset.
  2. Type your own details in the "You" fields once and click Save (saved only in this browser).
  3. For each new client, fill in the "Client & project" form. Pick Letter or A4, open a document tab and click Print / Save as PDF; choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
  4. Open the Emails tab, click Copy and paste into your own email. Track each client's steps in client-onboarding-checklist.xlsx.

Tip: turn off "Headers and footers" in the print window for a clean page. Clearing your browser data or switching browsers starts the form empty, so keep the PDFs you send.
