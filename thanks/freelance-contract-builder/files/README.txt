WHICH FILE TO OPEN FIRST
Open contract-builder.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari (double-click it). It works offline in any modern browser; no install and no account. Choose a Preset, fill in the four cards on the left, review the Agreement, Statement of Work and Change Order tabs, then click Print / Save as PDF. Chapter 1 of the guide walks you through it.

PRINTING
1. Choose Letter or A4 in the top bar.
2. Choose "Print all 3" (each document starts on a new page) or "Print this tab only".
3. Tick "Print notes" only if you want the plain-English notes on paper.
4. Click Print / Save as PDF. In the print window choose "Save as PDF" or your printer, and turn off the browser's own "Headers and footers" option.
In Chrome/Edge every printed page gets one footer line: "Not legal advice..." on the left and the agreement ref, document (SOW or CO number) and "Page x of y" on the right. Browsers without print page footers print the "Not legal advice" line at the end of each document instead.

PRIVACY
Everything you type, your logo and up to 20 saved contracts stay in your browser's local storage on this computer. The builder makes no network requests and loads no outside fonts or scripts. Clearing your browser data deletes saved contracts, so use Export JSON to keep a backup, and Import JSON to restore it or move it to another computer.
