How to use it

  1. Download contract-builder.html (or unzip the ZIP) and double-click it. It opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari and works offline; no install, no account.
  2. Choose a Preset (Design, Web/Shopify, Writing, Marketing retainer or Consulting) and fill in the four cards: You, Client, Project & scope, Fees & payment. Type your state or country under Governing law.
  3. Review the Agreement, Statement of Work and Change Order tabs. Pick Flexible, Standard or Firm wording per clause and switch on the plain-English notes to see what each clause protects you from.
  4. Choose Letter or A4, then Print / Save as PDF ("Print all 3" or "Print this tab only"). Turn off the browser's own "Headers and footers" option.
  5. Send it with Email 1 from contract-emails.txt. Guide Chapter 5 has replies for client pushback; Chapter 6 lists red flags in a client's own contract.
  6. Use Export JSON now and then: saved contracts live only in this browser, and clearing browser data deletes them.

Not legal advice; not written by a lawyer. Have a local lawyer review your agreement before you rely on it.
