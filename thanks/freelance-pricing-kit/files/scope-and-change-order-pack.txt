FREELANCE SCOPE & CHANGE ORDER PACK
Part of the Freelance Pricing & Quote Kit by Hustle Studio
Plain text: paste into Google Docs, Word, Notion or an email. Replace everything in [brackets].
Templates only. This is not legal advice; for large contracts, have a lawyer review your terms.

======================================================================
1. SCOPE OF WORK SECTION (paste into your proposal or quote)
======================================================================

SCOPE OF WORK
Project: [Project name]
Goal: [One sentence: what the finished work should achieve for the client.]

Included
- [Deliverable 1, with a number: e.g. "Design of 5 pages: Home, About, Services, Blog index, Contact"]
- [Deliverable 2: e.g. "Build in WordPress using the client's existing hosting"]
- [Deliverable 3: e.g. "Entry of client-supplied text and images"]
- Revisions: [2] rounds of consolidated feedback per deliverable
- Meetings: [1] kickoff call + [2] check-in calls of up to [30] minutes
- Support: [14] days of bug fixes after launch

Not included (available as add-ons on request)
- [Copywriting or editing of text]
- [Stock photos or paid fonts/plugins; billed at cost if needed]
- [Additional pages beyond the 5 listed]
- [Ongoing maintenance, hosting or updates after the support period]
- [SEO, ads or analytics setup]

Client responsibilities
- Provide [text, images, logins] by [date]
- Send consolidated feedback (one email per round) within [3] working days
- Delays in client materials or feedback move the delivery date by the same amount

Additional work
Anything not listed under "Included" is quoted separately before work starts,
at [rate]/hour or as a fixed price agreed in writing via a change order.
Rush work (delivery earlier than the agreed timeline) carries a [25]% fee.

Tip: aim for 3-5 "Not included" bullets. Pick the things clients in your
field most often assume are included.

======================================================================
2. KICKOFF EMAIL RECAP (send on day 1)
======================================================================

Subject: [Project]: kickoff recap and next steps

Hi [Name],

Great to get started. A quick recap so we're on the same page:

- What I'm delivering: [short list]
- Revisions: [2] rounds per deliverable, with feedback collected into one email per round
- Timeline: [milestone 1, date] → [milestone 2, date] → launch [date]
- What I need from you: [materials] by [date]
- Not included this time: [2-3 most likely assumptions]

Anything outside this list I'll quote separately before starting, so there
are never surprises on the invoice.

Looking forward to it,
[Your name]

3. CHANGE ORDER
======================================================================

CHANGE ORDER #[1]
Project: [Project name]                 Date: [date]
Client: [Client name]                   Original agreement: [proposal/contract, date]

Requested change
[One or two sentences describing exactly what is added or changed.]

Not included in this change
[e.g. "Copywriting for the new page"]

Estimate:         [X] hours incl. communication overhead
Price impact:     +[amount]     New project total: [amount]
Timeline impact:  [none / delivery moves from X to Y]
Payment:          [added to final invoice / 50% before work starts]

All other terms of the original agreement remain unchanged.

Approved by: ______________________   Date: ____________
(A reply email saying "approved" also counts as approval.)

How to price it: estimated hours × (1 + overhead %, typically 10-20%) × your
rate × rush multiplier (1.0 normal, 1.25 rush, 1.5 urgent). Round up. The
pricing-calculator.html in this kit gives you your rate.

4. CHANGE LOG (keep one per project; share it at each milestone)
======================================================================

#  | Date     | Request (who asked)          | In scope? | Hours | Price  | Status
---|----------|------------------------------|-----------|-------|--------|------------------
1  | [date]   | [e.g. Extra FAQ page - Sam]  | No        | 4     | [300]  | Approved CO #1
2  | [date]   | [e.g. Button colour - Sam]   | Small     | 0.25  | 0      | Done, absorbed (noted)
3  | [date]   | [ ... ]                      |           |       |        | Quoted / Declined

Why keep one: small "absorbed" favours become visible, so when they add up
you can bundle them into a single change order with evidence.

======================================================================
5. REPLY SCRIPTS (short versions)
======================================================================

A) Small favour you absorb
"Done! That one was outside our scope but quick, so no charge this time. For
anything else beyond scope I'll send a short quote first."

B) New feature
"Good idea. It isn't in the current scope, so two options: add it now for
[price] (delivery moves to [date]), or do it as phase 2 after launch so the
current date holds. Which do you prefer? I'll send a change order."

C) Extra revision round
"We've used the [2] included rounds. More rounds are [price] each. Reply
'go ahead' and I'll start today."

D) Deadline pulled earlier
"Options: full scope by [new date] with a [amount] rush fee; core items by
[new date] and the rest on the original date at no extra cost; or keep [date]."

E) Pile-up of small requests
"I've listed the extra requests from the past weeks: [list]. Together
they're about [X] hours. I suggest one change order for [price]. Once it's
approved, I'll fold them into the next delivery."

F) Saying it on a call
"Let me check how that fits the scope and come back to you by email today."
(Never agree to price or timeline live. Confirm in writing.)
