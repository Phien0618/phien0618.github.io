How to use it (30 minutes)

  1. Floor Rate tab: enter your income goal, costs, tax rate and an honest share of billable hours. The result is your floor rate.
  2. 3-Tier Quote tab: list the tasks of a real project and tag each one tier 1, 2 or 3 to get Essential, Standard and Premium prices and deposits.
  3. quote-template.html: paste in the three prices, edit the yellow fields and send it. Keep Chapter 4 open for when the client replies.
