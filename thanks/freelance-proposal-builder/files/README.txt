OPEN THIS FIRST
Double-click proposal-builder.html. It opens in your web browser and works
offline. Fill in "My profile", load the example for your field from the top
bar, replace the client details and prices, then click Print / Save as PDF.
The guide's Chapter 1 has a 5-minute walkthrough.

PRINTING (LETTER OR A4)
- In the builder, choose Letter or A4 in the top bar, then click
  Print / Save as PDF. In the print dialog choose "Save as PDF" or a printer,
  set the same paper size, margins "Default" and scale 100%.
- Turn on "Background graphics" so the option colours print.
- Print from Chrome or Edge to get your logo in the header of every page and
  "Page x of y" in the footer. Other browsers print the proposal without
  that header and footer.
- The guide PDFs print at 100% on Letter or A4. The checklist and worksheet
  are pages 22 and 23.

PRIVACY
Everything you type, your logo and your saved proposals stay in your own
browser (localStorage). The builder makes no network requests and uploads
nothing. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so use Export JSON to keep
a backup or to move to another computer, and Import JSON to restore.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Saved proposals: up to 20.
- Loading an example replaces the proposal you are editing (your profile,
  colour and logo stay). Save first if you want to keep it.
- All example clients, people and businesses are fictional. In "Why me", use
  your own real results only.
- This product is writing guidance, not legal advice. A proposal is not a
  contract: after the client says yes, send a short written agreement.
