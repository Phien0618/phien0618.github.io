FREELANCE PROPOSAL BUILDER: 8 FOLLOW-UP EMAILS
==============================================

Schedule: send Email 1 with the proposal (Day 0), then Day 2, Day 5, Day 10
and 1 day before the proposal expires. Emails 6-8 are replies to three answers
you may hear.

Replace every [Bracket]. In proposal-builder.html these are filled in for you:
[Client First Name], [Project Name], [Recommended Option], [Recommended Price],
[Valid Until], [Start Date], [Essential Option], [Essential Price], [Your Name].
Brackets such as [Time option 1] are yours to write.
