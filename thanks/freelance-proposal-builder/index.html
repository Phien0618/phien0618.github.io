A proposal in five minutes

  1. Double-click proposal-builder.html and fill in My profile: name, business, email, website, currency. Pick a brand colour and add your logo.
  2. Load an example from the top bar that matches your field. It fills all nine sections with a complete proposal you can edit.
  3. Replace the client details in section 01: project name, client, dates and the client's goal in their own words.
  4. Set your three prices and the deposit % in section 06. Deposits, balances and dates are calculated for you.
  5. Choose Letter or A4 and click Print / Save as PDF, then send it with Email 1 from the Follow-up emails panel.

Printing tips: in the print window choose "Save as PDF", the same paper size, margins "Default", scale 100% and turn on "Background graphics". Print from Chrome or Edge to get your logo in the header and "Page x of y" in the footer. Save each proposal (up to 20) before loading another example, and use Export JSON for backups; clearing browser data deletes saved proposals.
