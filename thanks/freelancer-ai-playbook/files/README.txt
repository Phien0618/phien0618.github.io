START HERE
1. Double-click prompt-builder.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox; desktop or phone).
2. Fill in "My Business" once (9 fields). Or press "Try the example profile (Sam)" to see how it works.
3. Pick a category and a prompt, fill the situation fields (client name, invoice #, amount...).
4. Press "Copy prompt" and paste it into your own ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any chat AI.
5. Read the draft, run the 3 checks under the prompt, edit, then send it yourself.
Empty fields stay in [square brackets] and show in yellow, so you can see what is missing.
"Copy blank template" copies the prompt with your profile filled in and every [field] left blank.
Read chapter 1 and 2 of the guide (about 10 minutes) for the method and the 5 mistakes the checks catch.

USING THE TXT OR CSV WITHOUT THE BUILDER
- Replace {{profile}} with your profile block (template at the top of prompt-library.txt and in guide chapter 1).
- Replace every [Square bracket] field with the real detail.
- Notion: Import > CSV, choose prompt-library.csv (creates a database with 120 rows).
- Google Sheets: File > Import > Upload, choose prompt-library.csv.
- Excel: File > Open, choose prompt-library.csv (plain ASCII, opens cleanly).

PRIVACY
The Prompt Builder makes no network requests and uploads nothing. Your profile, field values,
favourites and your own prompts are stored only in this browser (localStorage).
- "Export JSON" saves them to a file (backup, or to move to another computer or browser).
- "Import JSON" loads that file again.
- "Clear my data" deletes them from this browser.
Do not paste passwords, bank details or confidential client information into any AI chat.
