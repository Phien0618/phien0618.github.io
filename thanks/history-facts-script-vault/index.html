How to use it

  1. Open the PDF (Letter or A4, same content) and read “How to use” and “Accuracy & platform notes”.
  2. Pick a script from the index (S01–S20 are 30 s, S21–S40 are 45 s, S41–S60 are 60 s).
  3. Check the core fact against the reference on its “Verify with” line, and read the “Myth check” line.
  4. Change at least one of its 3 “Make it yours” points, then paste the voiceover into your TTS tool or record it yourself. Use the beat table (or script_beats.csv filtered by script_id) as your edit list: start/end seconds, visual, caption / pause and tone.
  5. Copy the “Ready to post” box into the upload screen: title, description, hashtags, cover text and the comment to pin.

To open the CSV files in Google Sheets: File → Import → Upload, separator Comma. In Excel: Data → From Text/CSV, File Origin “65001: Unicode (UTF-8)”. Full steps are in README.txt.

Use only images you have the rights to (public-domain paintings and maps, museum open-access images, your own drawings). You may use the scripts in your own videos, including monetized channels; you may not resell or share the files.
