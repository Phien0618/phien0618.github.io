How to use it

  1. Unzip the download and open the folder for your paper size. Read 00-START-HERE and 01-Host-Guide. The Host Guide never names the guilty party, so you can play too. Don't open 99-SOLUTION-Host-Only until all ballots are in.
  2. Use the casting table: 8 players use the 8 core characters; for 9–16 add optional characters in order; for 17–20 add Gossip Guests A–D. You play Inspector Sloane Whitaker.
  3. Send each guest their invitation: printed, phone E-Invite, or made in the Invitation Maker with your date, time and place.
  4. Print one booklet per player, the evidence cards and the Rumor cards at Actual size / 100%. Black and white is fine. Print file 99 last and seal it in an envelope without reading.
  5. On the night, follow the standard run sheet (about 2–2.5 hours) or the New Year's Eve run sheet (unmasking at about 11:30 pm).
