Step 7Printables from your guest list

Name tags (8 per page), place cards (6 per page), 12 award certificates and accusation ballots (8 per page), filled in with the names above. Choose your paper size, check the preview, then press Print. In the print window, set scale to 100% (Actual size) and turn headers and footers off.

Award winners

Pick a winner for each award to print their name on it, or leave it blank and write it in on the night. The party date fills in from your plan.

Step 8Backup & privacy

Your plan lives only in this browser's storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded. To move it to another computer or browser, export a backup file and import it there. Clearing your browser data also clears the planner, so export a backup first.
