How to use it

  1. Unzip the download and read 00-Start-Here.pdf (2 pages).
  2. Open the planner: double-click Murder-Mystery-Host-Planner.html. It opens in your browser, no internet or account needed.
  3. Plan: enter guest count, occasion, party date, start time and length. Your dated countdown checklist, run sheet and shopping list appear. Ticks and your own tasks save automatically in this browser.
  4. Add guests and characters: type your guests into the tracker, paste your game's character list (one name per line) and give each character to a guest.
  5. Print: choose Letter or A4 with the switch above the Print buttons, then print name tags, place cards, ballots and award certificates. Set scale to 100% ("Actual size").
  6. Prefer paper? Print the blank PDFs from the US-Letter or A4 folder at 100%. Black-and-white printing is fine.

Back up your guest list: it lives only in this browser on this device. Use "Export backup (.json)" in the planner to save a copy, and "Import backup" to move it to another computer.
