How to use it (5 minutes)

  1. Open the tool. Save Shorts-Script-Builder.html somewhere you'll find it (desktop, Documents, or your phone's Files app) and double-click or tap it. It opens in your browser and works offline. On a phone, if tapping the file does nothing, open it from your Files app and choose your browser.
  2. Build. Pick a niche, a hook type and a length (15, 30, 45 or 60 s), type your topic and press Build. Not sure what a finished script looks like? Press Load worked example.
  3. Time & Edit. Set your speed (130 / 150 / 170 / 180 wpm or custom; measure your voice or TTS once: read a 30-second script and double the word count) and rewrite each line. Type // for a 0.4 s pause. Red means the segment runs more than 10 percent long.
  4. Captions. Choose 1 to 5 words per caption or one per sentence, then Download .srt. The timings are estimates: nudge them after you add the voiceover.
  5. Library. Save the script as Draft, Recorded or Posted. Export CSV for your own tracker.

Back up your library. Saved scripts live in your browser on this device. Clearing browser data, or opening the file in another browser, starts with an empty library. Press Backup .json in the Library tab now and then, and Restore .json to bring everything back on any browser or device.

Updates and fixes. If you find a bug or something stops working after a browser update, email us. Fixed versions go on this same page, free for you.

The Quick-Start PDFs cover the same steps on 2 printable pages. Print the Hook Library and keep it next to your desk.
