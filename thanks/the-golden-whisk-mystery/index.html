How to use it

  1. Unzip the download and open the folder for your paper size. Read 00-START-HERE and 01-Host-Guide first; neither contains spoilers. Keep 99-SOLUTION-Grown-Ups-Only away from the kids until the reveal.
  2. Pick booklets for your group: 6 kids use 10–15 (the six core characters), then add 16, 17, 18 and so on in order up to 21 for 12 kids. For 13–16 kids, two kids share a character; for 14–24, use team mode in the Host Guide.
  3. Send the invitations (file 02, printed or the text version for parents) with each child's character and dress-up idea.
  4. Print at 100% / actual size. Start Here has the exact print list for your group. Black and white is fine. Cut out the Round 2 and Round 3 slips, fold them and close them with a seal (about 10 minutes for 6 kids), and cut the evidence, puzzle and hint cards.
  5. On the day: you play Chief Inspector Crumb and read Scripts 1–4. Three rounds of about 15 minutes each, then ballots, the ending from file 99 and the four awards.
