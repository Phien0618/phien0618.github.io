How to use it

  1. Unzip the download and open the folder for your paper size. Read 00-START-HERE and 01-Host-Guide. Don't open 99-SOLUTION-Host-Only until the reveal at 11:45 (or 8:45 for the Family Early Countdown).
  2. Use the casting table for your number of guests (6–16). The six core characters (booklets 10–15) are always used; add optional ones in order. You play Detective Rowan Hale.
  3. Invite each guest with a printed invitation, a phone E-Invite or a message from the Invitation Maker.
  4. Print at 100% / actual size. Print one 4-page booklet per guest single-sided, plus the 12 evidence cards, name tags and ballots. Black and white is fine. Print file 99 last, without reading it.
  5. On the night, follow your timetable: Midnight Edition (9:00 pm–12:00 am) or Family Early Countdown (6:30–9:00 pm).
