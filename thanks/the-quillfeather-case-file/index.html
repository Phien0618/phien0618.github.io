How to use it

  1. Print files 01–05 (28 pages) at 100% / "actual size". Black-and-white is fine. 00 and 99 are fine on screen; 06 and 97 are optional.
  2. Before you start: cut the hint cards (05) without reading the hint halves and fold them on the FOLD line. If you use 97 instead of the checker, put each page in its own envelope (labels in 06). Keep 97 and 99 out of sight.
  3. Read DI Moss's letter (01), meet the suspects (02), then work through the 18 documents (03), filling in the worksheets (04).
  4. Check each answer in Case-Checker.html or on the sealed page of 97. Stuck? Open Hint A, then B, then C.
  5. Read 99 last for the full story and the 7-step clue chain.

Two players take about 60–90 minutes; one player about 75–120 minutes.
