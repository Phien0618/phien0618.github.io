How to run it

  1. Open the folder for your paper size and read 00-START-HERE and 01-Host-Guide. If you need sign-off, send 02-Manager-Approval-One-Pager to your manager or HR. Don't open 99-SOLUTION-Host-Only until the reveal, unless you'd rather know the answer in advance.
  2. Find your head count in the Host Guide's casting table. The 6 core suspects (booklets 10–15) are always cast. Add optional suspects 16–19 in order, then deal guest cards from G1 and form 3–6 detective teams. You play Detective Sam Holloway.
  3. Send the invitations, using the printed cards or the email and Slack/Teams text, about a week before the party. Give each suspect their booklet in advance, and ask them to read pages 1 and 2 only.
  4. Print at Actual size / 100%. Black and white is fine. Start Here lists exactly what to print for 8, 12, 20, 30 and 40 players (61 to 96 pages). Cut the cards along the dashed lines.
  5. On the day, follow the Full Party timeline (2–2.5 hours) or the Lunch-Hour Express timeline (60–75 minutes) and read Scripts 1–4. Script 5, the reveal, is in the Solution file.
