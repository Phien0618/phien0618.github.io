How to use it

  1. Unzip the download and open the folder for your paper size. Read 00-START-HERE and 01-Host-Guide first; neither contains spoilers. Nobody opens 99-SOLUTION-Open-Last until everyone has voted after dessert, so the host can play and guess too.
  2. Pick booklets for your number of players: 4 players use 10–13, then add 14, 15, 16 and 17 in order for 5–8 players. The host takes booklet 10 or 11.
  3. Send each guest their invitation (file 02, printed or as the text version) about a week before, with their character and costume idea.
  4. Print at 100% / actual size, single-sided. Start Here has the exact print list for your party size (45 pages for 4 players, 66 for 8). Black and white is fine. Print file 99 without reading it, or keep it on a phone until the reveal.
  5. On the night: one round per course. Starter, main course and dessert each bring three evidence cards and a new booklet round. After dessert, everyone fills in a ballot, then a player opens the solution and reads the reveal aloud.
